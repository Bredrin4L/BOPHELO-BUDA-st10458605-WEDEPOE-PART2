## Project Overview
BUDA Auto Styling is a proposed local automotive styling and suspension shop focused on car enthusiasts who want quality suspension, wheels, alignment and car-care products.

The website is designed to present the business professionally, explain its services, showcase products and make it easy for customers to contact the shop.

## Part 1 Details

### Organization
**BUDA Auto Styling**

### Business Type
Local automotive styling, suspension and car-care shop.

### Mission
To provide quality automotive styling, suspension and wheel solutions while helping enthusiasts build safe, clean and personalised vehicles.

### Vision
To become a trusted local destination for automotive enthusiasts looking for quality products, professional workmanship and excellent service.

### Target Audience
- Car enthusiasts
- Stance and fitment enthusiasts
- Daily drivers
- Vehicle owners needing suspension work
- Customers looking for wheels and tyres
- Customers interested in car-care products

### Website Goals
1. Increase awareness of BUDA Auto Styling and its services.
2. Make it easier for customers to view services/products and contact the shop.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Number of contact/enquiry submissions
- Number of service enquiries
- Product/service page visits
- Mobile and desktop usability
- Time spent on the website

## Part 2 Details

### Main Features
- Multi-page navigation

- Mobile navigation

### Design Direction
The design uses a modern automotive aesthetic with a dark base, strong accent colour, high-contrast typography and large vehicle imagery. The layout uses CSS Grid and Flexbox.

### Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — About BUDA Auto Styling
- `services.html` — Suspension and automotive services
- `shop.html` — Wheels and car-care products
- `contact.html` — Contact and enquiry form
